Project DeepTech

For this exciting project, DBS is embarking on the creation of a new subsite dedicated to the burgeoning world of the metaverse. Collaborating closely with our in-house designer, our goal is to seamlessly transform creative concepts into a functional reality.

The most intriguing challenge encountered during this project centers around the styling of the timeline plugin. Achieving the desired look and feel demanded a meticulous approach, involving extensive CSS customization and overwriting to attain the precise visual aesthetics. Additionally, a significant portion of our efforts was dedicated to ensuring the responsiveness of this module across various devices and screen sizes.

While the technical aspects of this project may be relatively straightforward, its significance lies in our commitment to harnessing the potential of the metaverse to provide innovative solutions for our audience.
